Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 May 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how kartina uses cookies and similar technologies. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and advertising identifiers. We use them only as described below.

2. Who sets cookies?

  • Us (first party) — to remember your cookie consent choice.
  • Google (third party) — only if you accept non-essential cookies and ads are enabled (AdSense).
  • GitHub (hosting) — may use technical cookies or logs when delivering the site; see GitHub’s documentation.

3. Types of cookies we use

Strictly necessary (no consent required)

Name / storage Purpose Duration
kartina_cookie_consent (localStorage) Stores whether you accepted or rejected non-essential cookies Until you clear site data or change your choice

Advertising and analytics (consent required)

These are loaded only if you click Accept all on the cookie banner. If you reject non-essential cookies, we do not load Google AdSense.

Google may set cookies such as _gcl_*, IDE, or others for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement. Names and durations change over time; see Google’s cookie information.

4. Your choices

  • Reject non-essential — only the consent storage above is used; the barcode and ads stay hidden.
  • Accept all — unlocks the barcode, allows advertising cookies when ads are enabled.
  • Change your mind — use Cookie settings in the footer or clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • Browser controls — most browsers let you block or delete cookies in settings.

5. Legal basis

Under the GDPR and ePrivacy Directive, we rely on your consent for non-essential cookies. Strictly necessary storage for your consent record is used based on our legitimate interest in complying with cookie law.

6. Updates

We may update this policy when we add or change services (e.g. enabling ads). Check the “Last updated” date above.
